## Platform

web

## Users
Businesses, sales teams, call centers, and customer support operators in India needing automated, ultra-low latency voice agents that speak Hindi, English, and Hinglish.

## Product Purpose
GAP VoicePilot is a realtime AI phone agent platform that enables teams to build, configure, deploy, route, and monitor natural conversational voice calls for sales, lead qualification, customer support, and outbound campaign dispatch.

## Positioning
A direct streaming voice loop tuned for fast, regional language (Hindi, English, Hinglish) business calls with zero setup friction and full operational visibility into live call states, transcripts, and CRM sync.

## Operating Context
Web dashboard and marketing pages where operators configure AI assistants, attach phone numbers, run calling campaigns, view live call transcripts, and inspect platform capabilities.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Inbound & outbound AI voice calling with low latency speech-to-reasoning-to-speech.
- Native regional multi-language support (Hindi, English, Hinglish).
- Assistant builder with prompt tuning, fallback behavior, and call handoffs.
- Telephony management (business phone numbers, SIP routing, campaign dispatch).
- Realtime analytics, live transcripts, recordings, and CRM auto-sync.
